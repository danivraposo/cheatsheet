Windows Hardening & GPO

Configuracoes de seguranca local, diretivas de grupo, auditoria avancada e gestao de acessos remotos.

1. Politicas de Passwords e Account Lockout (PowerShell Rapido)

Aplicacao direta de politicas locais (Minimo 14 chars, 90 dias, 20 historico, bloqueio 5 tentativas em 15 min):

Comando net accounts 
net accounts /minpwlen:14 /maxpwage:90 /uniquepw:20 /lockoutthreshold:5 /lockoutduration:15 /lockoutwindow:15

Permitir execucao de scripts na sessao atual do PowerShell:

Bypass Temporario da Politica de Execucao 
Set-ExecutionPolicy -Scope Process Bypass -Force

2. Consolas Graficas de Gestao (GUI)

  • Local Security Policy: Tecla Win + R → digite secpol.msc
  • Active Directory Group Policy (no DC): Tecla Win + R → digite gpmc.msc

3. Navegacao em Consola (secpol.msc)

Password Policy

Caminho: Security Settings > Account Policies > Password Policy

  • Enforce password history: 20 passwords
  • Maximum password age: 90 days
  • Minimum password length: 14 characters
  • Password must meet complexity requirements: Enabled

Account Lockout Policy

Caminho: Security Settings > Account Policies > Account Lockout Policy

  • Account lockout threshold: 5 invalid logon attempts
  • Account lockout duration: 15 minutes
  • Reset account lockout counter after: 15 minutes

4. Auditoria e Logs do Windows (Advanced Audit Policy)

Caminho: Security Settings > Advanced Audit Policy Configuration > System Audit Policies

  • Policy Change: Audit Policy Change → Marcar Success e Failure
  • Privilege Use: Audit Sensitive Privilege Use → Marcar Success e Failure
  • Detailed Tracking: Audit Process Creation → Marcar Success e Failure

Consulta de Event IDs Uteis via PowerShell

Tentativas de Login Falhadas (Event ID 4625) 
Get-WinEvent -FilterHashtable @{LogName='Security'; Id=4625} -MaxEvents 5
Criacao de Utilizadores (Event ID 4720) 
Get-WinEvent -FilterHashtable @{LogName='Security'; Id=4720} -MaxEvents 5
Instalacao de Novos Servicos (Event ID 7045) 
Get-WinEvent -FilterHashtable @{LogName='System'; Id=7045} -MaxEvents 5

5. RBAC & Grupos Locais

Criacao de grupos e atribuicao de acessos remotos com menor privilegio:

Configurar Grupos e Acessos Locais 
# Criar Grupo e Utilizadores
New-LocalGroup -Name "Monitors"

# Adicionar Acessos Remotos e Privilegios (Jane: RDP | John: CLI/WinRM | Monitors: Performance)
Add-LocalGroupMember -Group "Remote Desktop Users" -Member "Jane"
Add-LocalGroupMember -Group "Remote Management Users" -Member "John"
Add-LocalGroupMember -Group "Performance Monitor Users" -Member "Monitors"
